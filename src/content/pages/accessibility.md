Adam the Realtor is committed to providing a website that is usable and welcoming for everyone.

## Our commitment

We work to make our website accessible to people with disabilities and to improve the experience for visitors using assistive technology, keyboard navigation, or different devices.

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. As we update our Southern California real estate resources, we review the experience and make practical improvements where possible.

## Need help using this site?

If you have trouble accessing information, using a feature, or completing a form, please contact Adam. We will do our best to provide the information or assistance you need in an accessible format.

## Share accessibility feedback

Your feedback helps us improve. Please include the page you were visiting, the issue you encountered, and the device or assistive technology you were using.
