## Key Real Estate Considerations When Buying in Westminster

Before making an offer, look at the complete cost and condition of the property—not just the advertised price.

### Understand the Total Cost of Ownership

In addition to your down payment and mortgage, account for closing costs, property taxes, homeowners insurance, maintenance, and any applicable HOA fees. If the property needs repairs or upgrades, include those expenses in your budget.

### Review the Condition of the Home

Some older properties may need updates to roofing, plumbing, electrical systems, windows, or other major components. Review the seller's disclosures and arrange appropriate inspections so you have a clearer picture of the property's condition.

### Compare Similar Homes

Look at recent comparable sales and current listings with similar locations, sizes, conditions, and features. Asking prices alone do not establish market value, and a property that appears inexpensive may require substantial repairs.

### Check Permits and Renovation History

If a home has an addition, converted garage, accessory dwelling unit, or major renovation, ask for relevant documentation and verify permit status with the appropriate authorities. Do not assume every improvement was completed or approved in the same way.

### Consider Future Flexibility

Think about whether the home's layout, location, maintenance requirements, and costs will continue to work for you. If you may sell or rent the property later, consider its broader appeal and check any rules that could affect your plans.
