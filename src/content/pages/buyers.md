## The Home Buying Process, Step by Step

Here's how buying a home works when we work together.

### 1. Free Buyer Consultation

We talk about your timeline, budget, neighborhoods and priorities. I'll explain how the home purchase process works and what to expect.

### 2. Get Pre-Approved for a Mortgage

Pre-approval shows you what you can afford and makes your offer stronger with sellers. I can connect you with trusted lenders, and you can use home affordability tools to see a realistic price range.

### 3. Search for Homes

You get tailored listings, tours and honest feedback. If a home has a problem, I'll say so.

### 4. Make an Offer

We study recent sales, decide on price and terms, and write an offer built to win without overextending you.

### 5. Negotiate and Open Escrow

Once the offer is accepted, escrow opens, your deposit is made and the disclosure documents begin.

### 6. Inspections and Appraisal

Inspections show the home's true condition, and I negotiate repairs or credits if issues come up. A third-party appraiser confirms the value for your lender.

### 7. Title, Final Loan Approval and Walkthrough

The title company clears title and coordinates with your lender. Before closing, we do a final walkthrough to confirm the home is in the agreed condition.

### 8. Closing Day

You sign the paperwork, funds transfer, and you get the keys.

## First-Time Home Buyer in Orange County

Buying your first home is a big step, and the questions are normal: How much do I need for a down payment? What are closing costs? How competitive will I be?

### What First-Time Buyers Should Know
- Get pre-approved before you start touring homes
- Budget for closing costs, inspections and moving expenses, not just the down payment
- Check HOA fees and property taxes, since they affect your monthly payment
- Ask about down payment assistance programs that may be available
- Keep your finances steady: avoid new debt or big purchases during escrow

## Buying a Luxury Home in Orange County

Luxury buyers in places like Newport Beach and Huntington Beach have different priorities: privacy, views, lot value, resale appeal and discreet transactions. I help you evaluate what you're really paying for, and what will hold its value.

### Factors to Consider
- Location and view, which drive long-term value
- Condition and quality of upgrades
- Insurance, property taxes and ongoing maintenance
- Off-market opportunities through agent connections

If you're buying to rent or build long-term wealth, the numbers matter more than the staging. We look at rental demand, expected rent versus expenses, financing and neighborhood trends, including multi-family homes. I can also help if you're interested in renting.
